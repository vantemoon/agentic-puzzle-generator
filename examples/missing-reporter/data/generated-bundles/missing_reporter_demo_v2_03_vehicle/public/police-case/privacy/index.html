Case portal privacy notice

How information sent through a case form is handled.

Use of submissions

Information sent through a case-specific form is associated with that case and reviewed by authorized personnel. Submission confirmations may show a next requested action.

Do not submit

  • Emergency reports
  • Unrequested account details
  • Material unrelated to the named case
