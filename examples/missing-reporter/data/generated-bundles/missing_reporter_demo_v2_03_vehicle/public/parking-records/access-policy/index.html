Record access policy

Scope and limits of the public parking lookup.

Published sessions

The lookup returns completed sessions when the user has the full parking reference. It does not provide open browsing by plate, customer, or payment account.

Data limits

  • Only completed sessions are available.
  • References must be entered in full.
  • Payment card details are never displayed.
