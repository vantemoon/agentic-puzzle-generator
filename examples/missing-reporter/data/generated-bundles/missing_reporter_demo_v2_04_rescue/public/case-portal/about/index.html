About the Case Portal

How River City Police accepts and reviews public case information.

Portal purpose

The portal gives authorized reporting parties access to selected case updates and secure evidence forms. It does not publish full case files.

Before sending information

  • Use only records you can identify.
  • Include exact locations when requested.
  • Do not contact a suspect or enter a private property.
