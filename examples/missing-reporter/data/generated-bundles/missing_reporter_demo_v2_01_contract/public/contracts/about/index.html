About Contract Search

Purpose and publication scope of the city contract database.

Public records service

River City Contract Search publishes city award records for public review. Records distinguish prime contractors, listed subcontractors, and related financial files when those details are available.

Included records

  • Award summaries
  • Vendor roles
  • Award dates
  • Related payment files
